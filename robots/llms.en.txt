# Referendum.social — Latest USA Polls

> Explore the newest public opinion polls in the United States, vote and see the results.

Browse questions on politics, government, people and world events. Poll pages show vote counts, charts and discussion; available filters compare age, sex, region and registration status. Responses come from self-selected participants, not a representative survey or official election.
