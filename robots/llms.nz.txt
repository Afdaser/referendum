# Referendum.social — Latest New Zealand Polls

> Explore the newest public opinion polls in New Zealand, vote and see the results.

Browse questions on government, relationships and religion. Poll pages show vote counts, charts and discussion; available filters compare age, sex, region and registration status. Responses come from self-selected participants, not a representative survey or official election.
