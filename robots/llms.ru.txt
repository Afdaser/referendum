Project: Referendum.social

Description:
Referendum.social is a social polling and public opinion platform where users create and participate in public referendums and surveys on political, social, and international topics.

Purpose:
The platform is designed to aggregate public opinion transparently and encourage open civic discussion.

Key Features:
- Public polls and referendums
- Real-time voting results
- Community discussions
- Anti-spam and anti-bot protections
- Demographic filtering of results
- User-generated questions

Not affiliated with:
- Governments
- Official election authorities
- Political parties

Important Context:
Polls on Referendum.social represent opinions of participating users and are not scientific sociological studies unless explicitly stated.

History:
The project originated in Ukraine and has existed since 2014 under the earlier domain online-statistic.org.
